## Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create a NEW clean professional 3D product illustration for the right side hero of the NARBE Trinkey setup website. Image 1 is REFERENCE ONLY for the real device shape, proportions and connector construction; do not reuse the photograph or wood surface. Image 2 is the exact NARBE Foundation logo to place centered on the enclosure, preserving its house/speech-bubble silhouette, three hearts above chimney, game controller, camera and screen. Render ONE small silver-gray rounded rectangular 3D-printed protective Trinkey enclosure with the purple/lavender logo on its top face, a metal USB-A male connector protruding from the RIGHT short side and a single black 3.5mm plug entering the LEFT short side. From that black plug a short Y-splitter with TWO flexible black cables curves toward the lower left, terminating in one red and one white female 3.5mm socket like the photo. No round external switch pads, no bare green circuit board, no invented controls or extra USB plugs. Camera three-quarter top view, enough perspective to show the enclosure thickness and USB connector. Faithful proportions: enclosure slightly wider than USB-A connector, compact almost-square rectangular top. Logo should look neatly printed into the top, not floating, original purple colors not blue/orange mascot. Scene: isolated studio composition on uniform pale sage #e9efe6 with soft natural contact shadows, beautifully lit technical product visualization. Landscape 3:2 framing, device at upper right center and two socket ends toward lower left, everything entirely inside frame with generous margins. No added text, labels, watermarks, hands, or scenery.
