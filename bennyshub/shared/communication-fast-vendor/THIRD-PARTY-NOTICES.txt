English female voice trained by Bryce Beattie on the public-domain LJ Speech
dataset (Keith Ito and Linda Johnson). See VOICE-MODEL-CARD.txt.
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

Runtime assets are downloaded only when Faster is chosen. Messages are processed
on this device. Only public runtime/model assets are cached persistently; speech
text and generated audio stay in memory. No cloud speech service or analytics
is added by this voice engine.
