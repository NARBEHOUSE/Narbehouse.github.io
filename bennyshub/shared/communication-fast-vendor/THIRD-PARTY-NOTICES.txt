The optional voice downloads from rhasspy/piper-voices at revision
c10ece1aade47bb51c153c893d14e5bf8e5b7117. Twelve English voices: eleven medium-quality models and one low-quality model.
The original six models:
- en_US-ljspeech-medium: LJ, female; public-domain LJ Speech dataset
  (Keith Ito and Linda Johnson). See VOICE-MODEL-CARD.txt.
- en_US-bryce-medium: Bryce, male; the contributor's own public-domain recordings.
- en_US-kristin-medium: Kristin, female; public-domain LibriVox recordings.
- en_US-norman-medium: Norman, male; public-domain LibriVox recordings.
- en_GB-cori-medium: Cori, female, UK; public-domain LibriVox recordings.
- en_US-john-medium: John, male; public-domain LibriVox recordings, fine-tuned
  from the Kristin voice.
These voice models were contributed by Bryce Beattie. See the corresponding
VOICE-<NAME>-MODEL-CARD.txt files for their complete training/provenance notices.
https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/
https://librivox.org
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

Additional models (downloaded unmodified from the same pinned revision):
- en_GB-alba-medium: Alba, female, Scottish English. University of Edinburgh
  Alba speech corpus: https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- en_GB-northern_english_male-medium: Northern English, male, UK.
- en_GB-southern_english_female-low: Southern English, female, UK (16 kHz).
  Both use the Google UK and Ireland English dialects corpus, OpenSLR 83:
  https://www.openslr.org/83/ — CC BY-SA 4.0 International:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium: Jenny (Dioco), female, Irish. Recorded by Jenny.
  https://github.com/dioco-group/jenny-tts-dataset
  Custom attribution terms permit commercial use; the interface credits
  Jenny (Dioco). See VOICE-JENNY-DATASET-NOTICE.txt for the complete terms.
- en_US-joe-medium and en_US-mike-medium: Joe and Mike, male, US English.
  Open Home Foundation Voice datasets: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/voice-datasets
  CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
See the corresponding VOICE-<NAME>-MODEL-CARD.txt files for training/provenance.
All six additional model cards state fine-tuning from the U.S. English Lessac
medium voice. These notices do not replace the upstream licenses.

Runtime assets are downloaded only when Faster is chosen. Messages are processed
on this device. Only public runtime/model assets are cached persistently; speech
text and generated audio stay in memory. No cloud speech service or analytics
is added by this voice engine.
