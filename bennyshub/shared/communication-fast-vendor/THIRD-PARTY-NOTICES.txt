The optional voice downloads from rhasspy/piper-voices at revision
c10ece1aade47bb51c153c893d14e5bf8e5b7117. Six medium-quality English models:
- en_US-ljspeech-medium: LJ, female; public-domain LJ Speech dataset
  (Keith Ito and Linda Johnson). See VOICE-MODEL-CARD.txt.
- en_US-bryce-medium: Bryce, male; the contributor's own public-domain recordings.
- en_US-kristin-medium: Kristin, female; public-domain LibriVox recordings.
- en_US-norman-medium: Norman, male; public-domain LibriVox recordings.
- en_GB-cori-medium: Cori, female, UK; public-domain LibriVox recordings.
- en_US-john-medium: John, male; public-domain LibriVox recordings, fine-tuned
  from the Kristin voice.
These voice models were contributed by Bryce Beattie. See the corresponding
VOICE-<NAME>-MODEL-CARD.txt files for their complete training/provenance notices.
https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/
https://librivox.org
https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices

Runtime assets are downloaded only when Faster is chosen. Messages are processed
on this device. Only public runtime/model assets are cached persistently; speech
text and generated audio stay in memory. No cloud speech service or analytics
is added by this voice engine.
