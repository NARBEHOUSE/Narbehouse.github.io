# Matchy Match library credits

Card pictures and sounds in `packs/` come from the sources below. Keep this file with the packs:
the Creative Commons licenses require the credit to travel with the files.

## Picture sources

- **ARASAAC pictograms by Sergio Palao, Government of Aragón (arasaac.org)**, [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/), found through OpenSymbols (opensymbols.org). 278 pictures.
- **Mulberry Symbols by Steve Lee (mulberrysymbols.org)**, [CC BY-SA 2.0 UK](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/uk/), found through OpenSymbols (opensymbols.org). 47 pictures.
- **Twemoji by Twitter, Inc. and contributors**, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), found through OpenSymbols (opensymbols.org). 29 pictures.
- **Made for NARBE** in the same flat style with ElevenLabs image generation (gpt-image-2), where no free symbol existed. 50 pictures.
- Pictures that were already in the packs before this expansion are not listed here.

Pictures carrying ARASAAC's `+s` plural mark had that mark removed (an adaptation, shared under the same license).
