❓ How to Play Benny's Blackjack

🎯 Blackjack Rules

  • Get as close to 21 as possible without going over (busting).
  • Aces count as 11 or 1 automatically to maximize your hand.
  • Face cards (Jack, Queen, King) count as 10.
  • Hit to draw another card. Stand to keep your hand.
  • When you stand, Dealer reveals their hole card and draws until reaching 17 or higher.
  • Tie goes to the player! If hand totals are equal, the player wins!

🕹️ Switch Controls

  • Two-Switch Mode: Tap Space to move highlight. Hold Space to scan backwards. Tap Enter to select.
  • One-Switch Mode (Auto Scan): Highlight advances automatically. Tap Enter to select current item.
  • In-Game Pause: Hold Enter or select the on-screen Pause button anytime.
  • Touch / Mouse: Tap or click any button directly.
