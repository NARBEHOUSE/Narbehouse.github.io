    Campaign review

    Check your goals and chapter order before saving or playtesting.

      Editor controls

      Power ball pegs charge a future shot. Burst and Multiball bonus pegs affect the current shot immediately. Add a reachable power near the top and targets below it, or hide a power behind a bumper for a rewarding bank shot. Playtest each layout to check the payoff and shot limit.

      Click or tap a peg to select it. Shift-click adds or removes a peg; drag empty space to box-select, or Shift-drag to add. Drag a selected peg to move the group. Scroll over a peg or block to resize it; selected pegs resize together. Right-click, press Shift+F10, or use Selection actions for more tools. Arrow keys move the selection; Shift moves 10 px. Ctrl+A selects all, Ctrl+D duplicates, Delete removes, and Ctrl+C/V copies/pastes. Use the peg list and coordinates without dragging.

      Select the base plate on the board or with Select base plate. Drag its ends to resize it. Left/right arrow keys move it; Shift moves 10 px. Plus and minus change its width. Right-click or Shift+F10 opens its settings menu. All edits can be undone.
