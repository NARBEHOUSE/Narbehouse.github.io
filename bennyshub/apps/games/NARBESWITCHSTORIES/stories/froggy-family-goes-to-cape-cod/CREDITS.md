The source PDF itself identifies its English edition as a modified version of the original story. This NARBE Switch Stories adaptation changes the title and setting to Cape Cod, simplifies the English text for switch access, adapts and extends the illustrations, replaces sign lettering, and adds character cutouts, switch-triggered animations, and image descriptions. Created to expand access to stories for people who use switches and support a wider range of accessibility needs. Artwork edits were made with the built-in image-generation tool, using the supplied original illustrations as references. No endorsement by the original contributors is implied.

The original book content and adapted illustrations in this folder remain subject to CC BY-NC 4.0, including its noncommercial restriction. A license covering application code does not replace this artwork/content license. Retain these credits and indicate further changes when redistributing an adaptation.

The in-game title page includes these attribution parties, the license link, source link, and a notice of changes. Its links are mouse/touch links excluded from switch scanning and keyboard tab order, and open externally without replacing the game.

## Page correspondence

The adaptation uses the separate PDF cover, numbered story pages 1–12 (physical PDF pages 2–13), and an added “The End” page using the cover illustration. Printed pages 2 and 11 repeat illustrations without prose; short original reading lines and switch actions have been added there. The publisher’s concluding pages supply the credits and are not additional reading pages.

`backgrounds/` and `sprites/` hold the adapted game artwork in optimized WebP format. The original PDF, extracted source images, full-size artwork and edit prompts are preserved in the local-only reference archive described in `../../ASSET-OPTIMIZATION.md`; they are not required to play the game.
