# Benny's Ballista

**Expanded campaigns:** Bramblewick, Coppercoast and Mossmere now have 24 levels each (72 total), with four seasonal chapters, dawn-to-night scenery, multi-building encounters, optional ammo caches, powder-depot objectives and late-game protection challenges. Dialogue describes the actual siege objectives, without bell/key recovery quests. See the [complete campaign catalogue](CAMPAIGNS.md).

**Siege feedback:** Material-specific splinters, stone chips, crystal shards and settling dust accompany progressively deeper damage cracks. Fire bolts shed embers and smoke, Splitters separate with a magical sparkle and three-note cue, Powder Bombs detonate and disappear on their first impact with a building or the ground, with expanding blast rings, and Boulders kick up grit while rolling. Nearby characters briefly recoil without moving their collision bodies. Layered local sound files vary impacts, add creaks and rubble tails, and reward chain collapses with a short musical flourish. The ballista crew now shares the castle characters’ rounded style, with teal uniforms and upright pennanted spears.

**Visual clarity:** Burning flames stay anchored, with upward soft embers instead of rotating sparks or fire rings. Masonry trim sits slightly outside wall faces to avoid flickering seams while characters patrol. Easy Aim uses plain spoken target descriptions, such as “Moss goblin on the left,” with separate visual labels.

**Effects controls:** Settings → Visual effects cycles Full, Gentle and Minimal. Minimal removes cosmetic particles; damage marks and essential burning indicators remain. Reduced camera movement also suppresses particles. Particle counts, dust and sound frequency are capped, cosmetic randomness is separate from physics, and aim cues stay prominent while new destruction sounds soften beneath speech and aim guidance.

**Character movement:** All 72 default levels have safe patrol routes. Characters walk and pause while choosing ammunition and during manual aim, including with reduced camera movement. Easy Aim target views hold their positions for reliable shots; visual-only breathing and small weight shifts keep stationary sentries alive. Narrow perches without a safe path remain stationary. The operating system’s reduced-motion preference suppresses idle animation.

**Custom campaigns:** Workshop → Campaigns creates a full adventure with up to 40 ordered levels. Name the campaign, write its opening and ending, add open or saved castles, duplicate/reorder stages, and edit each stage in the normal builder. Save and play from **Play Game → My Campaigns**, with independent progress, cumulative scores and collected ammo carried between levels. Campaign JSON includes all level contents and supports local files or public links.

**Portable custom castles:** Game and Workshop share My Castles. Import JSON files or public JSON links from either page, export castles or your saved library, and reopen imported copies without their original host. See [custom-level formats and hosting](CUSTOM_LEVELS.md).

**Scene builder:** Workshop → Rules & world → Scene offers 12 surroundings (meadow, woodland, harbor, river, lake, island, mountains, farmland, orchard, village, ruins and marsh), four seasons, five times of day and background props. Settings travel with saved/exported castles, drafts and undo. Each campaign uses its own environments and building footprints: timber villages and orchards, coastal harbors and islands, and forest roads and lakeside strongholds. Each progresses through all four seasons. Night scenes keep gameplay lighting readable; display contrast settings still apply.

**Campaign playability:** `tests/verify-playability-all.cjs` plays all 72 default levels through Easy Aim with real physics and limited ammo. Every guard must be defeated, and protected characters must survive. Starting Boulder supplies scale with enemy count (at least three); crates supply extra ammunition. Powder-depot stages include extra starting shots for the keg objectives.

**Story guidance:** Rowan enters on dry foreground ground, at least 30% closer to the ballista. Objective instructions are derived from the actual protected characters and prison bars in each build; the Workshop previews the same instructions. Bramblewick’s finale asks players to clear the tower, gate and courtyard guards; ammo crates remain optional.

**Protected characters:** princess, villager, fisher, healer and merchant have heart-shield markers. Choose “Protect friendly characters” in Story & goal; any friendly casualty fails the level, including fire, debris and rolling Boulder damage. The last seven levels of each campaign include this challenge. Their friendlies stand in a foreground clearance area. New multi-building encounters include roadside backstops to intercept incidental rubble while keeping friends visible from the ballista. Castles and Workshop templates support up to 24 depth layers to include this space.

**Destruction:** Roofs and beams fracture on hard contacts, including rotating edges hitting the ground. Timber sheds spinning splinters; masonry sheds chunks. Fragments inherit the broken piece’s movement and can damage nearby structures as well as characters. Damage clouds spread across wide pieces, and weakened supports can start a chain collapse. Contact damage is shared with the Workshop stability test. Quiet resting buildings stay intact, with limits on rubble count and collateral damage.

**Physics balance:** projectile knockback scales down with a structural piece’s volume, so long merged beams and heavy walls topple instead of launching at the same speed as a loose brick. Direct damage, fire, debris and friendly protection rules still apply.

**Camera controls:** The first shot opens on the ballista and its crew; starting manual aim with Space, Enter or the pointer brings the castle into view. Mouse wheel zooms the view. Hold right-click and drag to look around; release to restore the normal aiming view while keeping the selected zoom. Zoom beside Pause offers Zoom in, Zoom out, Ballista view, Aim view and Back, with Space/Enter and Auto Scan support. It is also available from the hold-Enter pause menu when aiming. Zoom is bounded from 55% to 165%, resets for each level, and does not change shots or physics.

**Camera framing:** small builds get up to 18% more aiming distance, a fixed manual aim view and a little more room in charging/Easy Aim views. Larger castles retain their close framing. Aiming and Explore fit the actual placed buildings, guards, crates and protected characters, rather than the width of an empty editor grid. This brings large late-campaign castles closer and works automatically for custom castles. Holding Enter to charge eases closer to the predicted first impact. Mouse movement and mouse-button charging keep the camera fixed so the scene stays under the cursor. After a hit, the camera frames the damage area and ground below, expands to include spreading destruction and additional projectile impacts, and holds the scene through results. Reduced camera movement keeps aiming steady and cuts to a fixed impact view, without an orbit or shake.

**Shot feedback:** Boulder lands with one solid thud and a fading rumble, without a repeated rolling/revving cue. Boulder has 25% more direct damage, a small landing impact area, and up to six seconds of bouncing/rolling damage with visible rotation and speed loss.

Menus open with no switch selection (index -1). Release Space to highlight the first choice, then Enter to select; AutoScan advances automatically after its interval. Mouse and touch can select directly. Keyboard aiming and charging are 30% slower (4.2 degrees/second and 17.5% power/second). Holding Enter pauses after seven seconds while manually charging, leaving time to reach full power; elsewhere the delay is five seconds. Out of shots offers the castle retry before continuing with unlimited shots.

Main menu: **Play Game / Settings / How to play / Exit**. Play Game offers **Bramblewick, Coppercoast and Mossmere**: three independent 24-level campaigns. Each grows from huts and a village through a manor, gatehouse, keep and fortress to a moated castle. Selecting a kingdom shows **Start campaign / Explore / Back**, or **Continue campaign** for an unfinished run. Starting a default level brings Rowan into the field, moves the camera closer, then shows a speech bubble and reads the dialogue. Long stories advance automatically through successive speech bubbles after each spoken passage, with reading time when speech is off. **Play level / Back** appear after the complete dialogue; **Skip intro** is always available through switches, mouse and touch. **Next level** goes directly to the next castle’s narrator, then gameplay, without a Start level / Explore / Back screen. Levels with their intro disabled open gameplay immediately. Kingdom selection returns at the finale. Explore is also available from Pause and animates authored patrol routes without changing the active castle, shots or score. Returning restores live character positions. Castle Workshop is in Settings behind the switch-access warning. Pause has **Continue / Explore level / Settings / How to play / Restart castle / Main Menu**, plus **Change ammunition** while manually aiming; both Settings entry points use the same options.

Campaigns with saved progress offer **Restart progress**, with a Cancel-first confirmation that clears only that kingdom’s checkpoints, scores, stars and ammo. **Settings → Reset all campaigns** confirms before clearing every campaign, including legacy scores; it ends the active game and returns to the main menu. Both resets preserve settings and Workshop castles.

Each kingdom has its own story and ending. Progress, collected ammunition and best results are saved independently per kingdom. The kingdom score sums one best result per level (higher points, then fewer shots), so retries improve a score without duplicating it. Results use natural speech and the finale reports total points and shots. Version-1 settings, score and stars remain legacy data. Version-2 campaign saves migrate once to the expanded checkpoints while retaining their scores, stars and ammo; completed short campaigns resume at the first added encounter. In the Workshop, **Rules & world → Castle** names a custom level; **Story & goal** sets its dialogue, ending and objective, and can enable or disable the narrator’s scene. **Narrator** sets a custom name and chooses Rowan’s style, an older man or a woman. **Mission goals** selects every guard, crowned guards only or no guard requirement, plus destruction of selected object types and collection of selected ammo types. All selected requirements must be completed; collection requires hitting a crate during this attempt. Friendly protection is a separate rule.

Optional **Protect friendly characters** levels require at least one princess, villager, fisher, healer or merchant block. A ready-made Princess prison is in the structure palette. Complete the selected mission goals, break prison bars when present, and keep every protected character safe; a projectile, blast or falling-block hit requires restarting. Ordinary castle-clearing levels keep their existing rules. Stories, endings and objectives travel with saved castles, imported courses and editor drafts.

The current game uses the scene itself for aiming, with no lower shooting menu. Before each shot, a compact side selector shows ammunition: Space cycles it and Enter chooses it. Pause stays in the bottom left. Hold Enter while manually aiming to reach Pause and Change ammunition; Easy Aim keeps Change ammunition in the bottom right. Menus show all choices in a responsive grid without pagination or scrolling.

There are two aiming styles. **Easy aim** is a saved game setting; **Auto Scan** controls whether highlights advance automatically:
- **Easy aim off, Auto Scan off:** hold Space to sweep aim; release to stop. Each new press reverses direction. Hold Enter to charge range; release to fire. Space and Enter can be held together, in either order, to keep aiming during the charge. Continue holding Enter to pause without firing. Mouse movement points at the actual castle surface under the cursor. Hold the left mouse button to charge range and keep moving the mouse to adjust direction without resetting charge; release to fire. Touch players can drag to aim/range and tap the aiming ring to fire. A small ring at the aiming point shows charge.
- **Easy aim on:** up to five close-up camera views show strategic hit areas, without circles covering the castle. Space moves the highlight on release and Enter fires on release, without holding to aim or charge. Targets prioritize ammo crates, crowned guards, powder kegs and load-bearing supports; cover is prioritized when it blocks a key target, so unrelated supports cannot crowd the remaining objective out of the scan. Suggestions use the current projectile’s real trajectory and refresh after each shot. Mouse/touch arrows change the view; the compact Fire button fires at the current target.
- **Auto Scan on:** highlights advance automatically through targets, Pause and Change Ammo. Enter alone plays. Auto Scan always supplies target aiming for one-switch access; turning it off returns to the saved Easy aim preference.

**Aim sounds** adds faster ticks near objectives, a double chime for a direct objective hit, and a low warning when a rescue shot hits a protected character. **Camera movement** reduces animation and shake; Easy Aim changes views immediately in reduced-motion mode. Shared TTS, high contrast and unlimited shots remain available.

**Every fresh campaign starts with Boulder only**, including replays of completed kingdoms. Unfinished campaigns keep their own inventory; switching kingdoms never transfers ammo. In **limited mode**, each default level begins with at least three Boulder shots, scaling with enemy count; destroying a crate adds **4 Stone Bolts, 3 Fire Bolts, 3 Splitters or 2 Bombs** for that level. In **unlimited mode**, a collected ammo type stays unlimited for the rest of that kingdom. Crates award ammo when destroyed, including by collateral damage. A large camera-facing emblem coin identifies each crate; collecting it shows an emblem notice and plays a pickup fanfare during the action. The ballista’s loaded projectile matches the selected ammo and disappears when fired. Workshop crates are `L`, `D`, `Y`, `Z`; the starting Boulder count is configurable in Castle rules.

**Fire Bolts** ignite an area, progressively char and damage components, and spread through touching timber. Fire is bounded in duration and pauses with gameplay. Characters touching burning pieces catch fire and are defeated quickly. **Powder kegs** create a damaging blast, ignite the surrounding area and chain into nearby kegs. Whole building pieces and fractured debris damage characters through physical contact; substantial character falls are also lethal. Nearby guards carry buckets in front as they walk, stop and face the fire, then tip the bucket and splash water from its rim. Static guards also turn toward nearby fires. Their reaction is visual and never cancels a successful fire attack.

Default castles have supported **character patrols**. Characters wait for varied intervals, walk with moving feet, turn toward travel and face forward at stops. Paths pause while an Easy Aim target is selected; a toppled character never snaps back to its path. In the Workshop, choose **Character paths**, click a guard or crowned rascal, then mark up to eight stops on the top-view map. Blocked or unsupported routes are disabled; **Keep static** removes that character’s patrol. Paths persist through saves, course imports and undo. Save/Playtest checks that later edits have not blocked a route or removed its floor, and identifies any route that needs repair. Choose Playtest → Explore to watch the routes. Glass windows and crystal are transparent with visible edges, so characters inside remain visible.

The Splitter opens partway through its arc into three separate projectiles. Each has its own trajectory and impact. A full volley costs one shot, and the turn waits for every fragment.

The **Castle Workshop** has a categorized, searchable, paged pop-out toybox, collectible ammo crates, additional materials/characters and fifteen ready-made structures. Wheel cycles blocks; Ctrl+wheel zooms. Drag to orbit, Shift+drag to pan, right-click to erase. R rotates a structure and T tips it.

To create reusable templates: choose **Select structure**, drag around the structure or click to add/remove cells through its full depth; Shift-drag adds another region, then **Save template**. Name the selection and find it under **Blocks & pieces → My templates**. Templates support import/export and placement with undo/redo. **Share** exports castle JSON, the saved course, or the template shelf. **Save** adds a castle to **My Castles** in the game; **Playtest** saves and launches it. Drafts recover locally. The editor requires a mouse/keyboard and has a spoken entry warning in the switch-accessible game.

All castle/character meshes and textures are procedural local Three.js assets. Sound cues and the medieval-style music loop are generated locally by `tests/generate-audio.cjs`, `tests/generate-aim-audio.cjs` and `tests/generate-siege-audio.cjs` and played through the hub's SafeAudio path. No model service or paid voice API is required at runtime.

## Third-party code

`js/three.min.js` is vendored unmodified from [three.js](https://threejs.org/)
r155 (MIT licence). `js/ammo.js` is vendored unmodified from
[ammo.js](https://github.com/kripken/ammo.js/) (zlib licence), a port of
[Bullet Physics](https://pybullet.org/) to JavaScript. Don't hand-edit
either; pull a fresh build from upstream if either needs updating.

Generated screenshots and reports in `tests/artifacts/` are disposable and ignored by Git. Keep the test scripts and audio generators; the runtime audio files in `audio/generated/` must ship with the game.

Startup builds only the saved castle. Full campaign physics checks are developer-only: open `index.html?audit=levels` to settle and validate all 72 castles before the menu. Normal launches still validate level ammo data. `tests/verify-startup.cjs` measures three menu loads and verifies a real opening shot; set `BALLISTA_AUDIT=1` to verify the opt-in audit instead, and `BALLISTA_BENCH_LABEL` to name the timing report. Local tests reduced menu loading from about 8 seconds to 2.4 seconds; browser, device and download times vary.

Fire uses larger, surface-anchored flame clusters and bounded smoke plumes with smooth fading. Damage and spread rates are unchanged. Damage cracks follow actual roof slopes and curved pieces instead of their physics boxes. `tests/verify-fire-surfaces.cjs` checks surface placement, live roof fires, damage parity across effects settings, particle limits and cleanup.
