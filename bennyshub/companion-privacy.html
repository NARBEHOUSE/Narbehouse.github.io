Benny's Hub and Companion privacy

Updated September 27, 2026 · NARBE LLC

This policy covers the Benny's Hub website and Benny's Hub Companion for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. The Companion provides switch-accessible playback controls and optional calendar and news connections. The Companion has no advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated user account system. External services used by the website have their own data practices, described below.

Information used on your device

  • Playback: the Companion uses URLs and tab/window identifiers for streams launched from the Hub, media elements, player controls, playback state and switch input. This lets it operate the player, return to your Hub and update supported episode links. It does not collect a general browser history. When a streaming service shows profile choices, the Companion can display those names in its control bar so you can select a profile. It does not store the profile names.
  • Preferences: scan timing, input sensitivity, scan mode, voice preferences and source permissions are used to personalize controls. Temporary playback and scan state is stored in browser session storage. News preferences and an optional calendar address use extension local storage; they are not synchronized through Chrome or Edge account sync by this extension.
  • Calendar: if you connect a private Google Calendar iCal address, the extension stores that address locally and retrieves the calendar from Google. The address is an access credential. Calendar data can contain names, event descriptions, locations and other personal information. The extension processes it to display your week in Day Hub. It does not send your calendar to NARBE LLC.
  • News: when enabled, the extension retrieves public feeds. An optional place name supplied for local news is sent to Google News as a search term.

Website storage and external services

The Hub website stores journal entries, streaming titles, episode links, recent playback, search history, personal vocabulary and settings in that browser's website storage. These are separate from extension storage. The public installation starts with an empty streaming library and journal. Exported backups go to the location you choose.

Keyboard predictions use a bundled local dictionary and words and phrases learned on your device. Typed prediction text is not sent to an AI service. Speech output uses your browser or operating system's selected voice; those voice services may have their own network and privacy behavior.

Optional weather searches send a place name to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, and weather requests send the selected coordinates to Open-Meteo. Optional movie metadata searches send titles and metadata identifiers through NARBE LLC's Cloudflare Worker to TMDB. No shared TMDB credential is delivered to the browser. Image requests go to the relevant image host.

YouTube Search uses YouTube API Services. When you search, your search terms and a verification token are sent to NARBE LLC's YouTube search service hosted on Cloudflare; that service retrieves results from YouTube. Google receives requests for player software, thumbnails and videos. YouTube may serve advertisements and use cookies or similar technology on your device. These are external services, even though the keyboard's predictions run locally. See the Google Privacy Policy. By using YouTube Search, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

YouTube Search uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated abuse. Cloudflare processes browser, device and connection information, including IP address, to issue a verification token. See the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Policy. The first-use privacy choice is stored locally. You can stop using the feature and clear site data to remove that choice and local search history.

Streaming services receive the video links you open and handle their own sign-ins, cookies, playback and subscriptions. The Companion does not read account passwords or authentication cookies. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, feed providers, media services and hosts of fonts, images and software libraries receive normal connection information such as your IP address and requested resource. Their policies apply to their services.

The supplied TMDB Worker configuration disables application request logging and observability, and uses request information for rate limiting and public metadata caching. This statement applies to the TMDB Worker, not the separate YouTube search service or infrastructure providers. Service providers may retain operational or security records under their own policies. Do not include private information in public search requests. Contact us with questions about a request sent to our services.

Use and sharing

Information is used only to provide the features you choose. NARBE LLC does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, use it for credit decisions, or transfer it for unrelated purposes. The extension has no endpoint that uploads your journals, calendar contents, switch history or personal library to NARBE LLC. Data may be sent directly to the services described above when you use those features.

The use of information received through browser APIs is limited to the Companion's stated functionality and follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and Microsoft Edge Add-ons data-use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

Enable or revoke individual sources in Companion settings. Remove a calendar there to delete its stored address and revoke the extension's calendar access; invalidate a shared private calendar link in Google Calendar if you need to revoke the link itself. Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage; temporary session state is cleared when the browser session ends. Local website data remains until you clear it or the browser removes it. To remove the website's saved data, use Hub Settings → My data, or clear site data in your browser. Clearing all site data also resets the YouTube privacy choice. Removing the extension does not remove website storage or exported backup files. Export important entries before clearing storage. The website's offline cache contains public application assets, not authenticated feeds or streaming video. Clearing local data does not delete records held by third-party services; use their privacy controls for that.
