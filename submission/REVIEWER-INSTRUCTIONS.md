# Reviewer instructions

Prepared instructions for the local Companion 1.0.8 candidate; it has not been submitted or approved. On October 4, 2026, the owner reported 1.0.7 submitted and awaiting approval, with 1.0.5 public; that historical publisher-dashboard status has not been independently verified. These steps describe the 1.0.8 package. The update fixes Netflix framing where provider centering transforms could leave only a quarter of the fitted picture visible. It adds no permissions, and previous release archives remain unchanged. Parking, Space brake and speech-wait settings are centralized in Hub Settings.

No Hub account, Python helper or Electron app is needed. Streaming provider accounts remain separate; use a public YouTube video to review core behavior without a paid account. No private credentials are supplied or required in these notes.

1. Install the ZIP through the store's review process. Open the website and refresh if it was already open. Dismiss the initial settings prompt if shown.
2. Open Companion & data at the top, then Companion settings. Turn on **Streaming and news** and approve the optional host permissions for the listed streaming services and news feeds. Companion setup has installation and connection help.
3. Return to Tools → Streaming → Settings → Open Editor. Expand the add-title form. Enter a test title and any public YouTube watch URL of your choice, choose Movies and a genre, then save. The public catalog deliberately starts empty; no media is bundled.
4. Return to Streaming, refresh it to load the saved title if needed, browse the title and choose Play. A dedicated fullscreen browser window opens with the video above a separate Companion control area. Check that controls do not cover the picture or captions, including after resizing, opening Help, and entering or leaving native fullscreen. On Netflix, verify that the whole picture stays inside the frame instead of shifting and showing only a quarter; captions should retain their normal placement. If a provider enters fullscreen on the video element alone, the Companion should return to the framed browser window without restarting playback; verify that both mouse clicks and switch input still operate the dock. A later Fullscreen selection can use the existing player wrapper. Also verify that Unlock browser releases the page even if a fullscreen transition is still finishing. This is especially important for live signed-in Netflix and Disney players.
5. In Step mode the bar starts with no highlighted control. Enter at that blank step does nothing. Space moves to Play / Pause; Enter activates the highlighted control. Hold Space for three seconds to scan backward. Check Play / Pause, volume, mute, seek on a seekable video, and Return to Hub. Return to Hub is the last control; the next scan step is blank before the controls repeat. Selecting Return to Hub should close the managed player and focus the original Hub without adding duplicates.
6. In Hub Settings, verify ordinary page scrolling with no nested panel scrollbar. All Scan options should remain visible. Scan Speed remains enabled with Auto Off and controls the backward-scanning interval. Parking, Space brake and Wait for speech are disabled until Auto is On; loop count also requires Auto park. Disabled choices should be skipped by switch scanning and retain their saved values. Input sensitivity remains available in either mode, and changing an available option keeps that control highlighted. Enter Settings afresh and check that scanning starts blank. Change scan speed and input sensitivity in the Hub's Scan Manager. The running player picks up these preferences. Enable Auto scan: with Parking off, scanning repeats through the blank step. Enter at the blank step does nothing unless Park when chosen is enabled; that option parks the scan. Auto park stops after 1, 2 or 3 complete loops, as chosen in the Hub. Enter while parked resumes at Play / Pause without activating it. With Space brake on, press Space to freeze the highlight; a short release leaves scanning paused, a second tap resumes, and a hold of at least three seconds resumes on release. A press at the blank step or while parked does nothing. Optional speech waiting gives a full scan interval after each spoken label completes. Scan pause uses only a dotted outline on the selected control, without a Paused label or badge. With speech enabled, Paused follows the current label without interrupting it; resuming cancels a pending notice. Actual parking clears every choice and brings the existing Parked status into view. The recurring blank step is silent in Step mode and in Auto with Parking Off.
7. Accidental clicks on the video should keep switch keys on the bar. Alt+Shift+B temporarily permits ordinary website interaction; press it again to resume the bar. This is needed for provider sign-in and mouse/keyboard interaction.
8. The **Streaming and news** switch in Companion settings turns all listed streaming services and news off together and revokes their host access. News retrieves public feeds only when enabled. Calendar is optional and accepts the user's own Google Calendar private iCal address. Removing the calendar clears that address and its permission.
9. The extension action opens its settings page. Scripts do not execute remote code, bypass DRM, or control unrelated provider tabs.

Screenshots use the real Companion UI with clearly labeled demonstration playback and no personal movie catalog, sign-in information, calendar or journal entries. Provider-specific previous/next actions are available only where the provider exposes suitable controls.

Open **Help & shortcuts** during playback: the video should pause. **Say I need help** uses browser speech even with spoken scan labels disabled. **Back to video** leaves playback paused. **Open keyboard**, **Open phrase board**, and **Hub main menu** close the managed streaming window and navigate the original Hub tab directly without adding another Hub. No Messenger or personal care phrases are included.

In Help, **Toolbar Speech On/Off** controls automatic Companion narration, persists for this browser profile and synchronizes across managed player windows. It does not change Hub voice settings or video mute. The explicit **Say I need help** action still speaks when Toolbar Speech is Off.
