Build With NARBE House

Helping you bring accessible game ideas to life.

Everything we make started with our brother Ben. He didn't have access to games he could actually play, so we built them, and we released them all for free.

For Ben, these games aren't therapy. They're fun, independence and a real part of his quality of life, and everyone deserves that. We design every game for minimal inputs, so anyone using one or two switches can play. We build for all kinds of players, from simple cause-and-effect games to games for teens and adults. They're made for everyone to experience and enjoy, and many people also find them useful in therapy sessions, clinics and classrooms.

We're not an AAC provider, clinicians or medical professionals. What we are is builders, and we want to work alongside SLPs, OTs, AT specialists and educators to create the games and experiences that don't exist yet. We want to be the place where your ideas come to life.

The Build for One Award

A few times a year, we choose a teacher, therapist, family or organization in our community and build them a game for free. It's built for one person, then shared freely with everyone.

For organizations: sponsor your own game

Sponsor a game built specifically for your organization, with your branding. We can make it a team experience: we'll host a one- or two-hour session with your team, come up with ideas together and build the game live. Once everyone's happy with it, we release it free on Switched Games with your logo and a link to your organization. As the site grows, so does your game's reach, and your community can share it too.
